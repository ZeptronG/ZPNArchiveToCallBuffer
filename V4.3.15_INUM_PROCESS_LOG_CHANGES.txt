ZPN - Archive to Call Buffer V4.3.15

- INUM values are emitted as Excel text cells so all 15 digits display instead
  of scientific notation such as 4.93001E+14.
- At the end of every run, including Stop, Proccess.log is written to the chosen
  Log folder.
- Proccess.log lists completed TAR files, TAR files active when interrupted, and
  every discovered TAR that still needs processing.
- The final inventory re-scans Source after cancellation so files that had not
  reached the bounded worker queue are included as NOT_STARTED.
- Cancellation now awaits the producer and all fixed workers before final logs
  are closed and the inventory is generated.
