ZPN - Archive to Call Buffer V4.3.19

- TAR Browser supports selecting and extracting multiple regular files at once.
- All selected entries are extracted during one sequential TAR pass.
- Existing output files can be replaced as a group, skipped as a group, or cancelled.
- Conflicting selected entries with the same output basename are detected first.
- New operational CSV files are named Audiolog_yyyyMMdd_HHmmss.csv.
- Resume and clear-old-log behavior remains compatible with legacy VerintAudio logs.
